Our Story

The name on the door of this site is Eden Manufacturing & Robotics. The shop is Eden 3D, in Toledo, founded by Max Sikorski.

Print farm. Short runs and one-off production prints out of Toledo.

Custom robotics and parts. Housings, fixtures, and machine pieces — designed around the job and printed in the material that fits.

The meetup. A weekly Midwest 3D printing club: news, show-and-tell, and shop talk. Weekly news and the Meetup group are both open.

What We Build

Print-farm runs and custom robotics parts from the Toledo shop. The hardware includes Voron-style tool-changers, in materials such as PPA carbon fiber, PEI, and TPU.
